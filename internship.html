Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Zorario PropTech Internship Program.

Is this a paid internship?

This is an unpaid, high-impact experiential learning internship program designed for ambitious students and freshers. You will work on real production systems alongside startup leadership, receiving 1-on-1 mentorship, an official verifiable Certificate of Completion, a Founder Letter of Recommendation (LOR), and direct fast-track Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) for full-time roles upon graduation.

Is this internship 100% remote (Work From Home)?

Yes, absolutely! All tracks in the Zorario Internship Program are 100% Remote / Work From Home (WFH). You can work from your college hostel, room, or anywhere across India with flexible daily hours (15–20 hours/week) designed to easily fit around your college schedule and exams.

What is the Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) process?

Throughout your internship, your sprint performance and initiative are evaluated. Top performers in every batch are offered full-time software engineering, marketing, or operations roles at Zorario before they graduate.

Will I receive a verified certificate and recommendation letter?

Yes. Upon successful completion of your internship tenure and milestones, you will receive an official verifiable Certificate of Completion and a personalized Letter of Recommendation (LOR) from Zorario's leadership. Every certificate contains a unique ID and QR code verifiable on our Certificate Verification Portal.

How long does the selection process take after applying?

Our recruitment team reviews every application within 24 to 48 hours. Shortlisted candidates are invited for a 30-minute virtual technical/introductory interview, with offers made within 3 business days.
